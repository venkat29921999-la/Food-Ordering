Our story

It started with a cold plate of fries.

The route

From one stove to a thousand doorsteps.

Scroll and follow our rider — every stop is a chapter of how Stackly got here.

2019

A napkin and a frustration

Two friends, one slow food app, and a napkin sketch of how ordering should feel. The first prototype shipped from a borrowed kitchen table.

2021

The first night rider

Twelve kitchens said yes, and one scooter took to the streets after dark. We learned every shortcut the hard way — and never stopped mapping.

2024

Smart routing, warm doorsteps

Our routing engine learned to send the nearest rider the second a dish hits the pass. Hot food stayed hot, and 98% of orders arrived on time.

You are here · 2026

1,200+ kitchens and growing

The route never ends — the next stop is your table. Come and taste the city with us.
